void LV_ATTRIBUTE_FAST_MEM lv_draw_sw_blend_color_to_rgb565_swapped ( lv_draw_sw_blend_fill_dsc_t * dsc)

Fill an area with a color. Supports normal fill, fill with opacity, fill with mask, and fill with mask and opacity. dest_buf and color have native color depth. (RGB565, RGB888, XRGB8888) The background (dest_buf) cannot have alpha channel
